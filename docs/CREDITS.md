# Credits

## 3D models

The robotaxis are built from these Sketchfab models, used under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Both were modified: each source is only used for its shape. Its side profile, sections
and plan were measured and traced into a few straight lines, and a new hard-surface body
was built from those flat planes, so no logo, badge, name, licence plate, panel gap or
other detail of the source survives; the body is given cartoon proportions (shorter
overhangs, more height, bigger wheels), flat colour blocks, and new wheels and details.
They are parodies and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Jaguar or Waymo.
