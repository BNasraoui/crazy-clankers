# Credits

## 3D models

The robotaxis are built from these Sketchfab models, used under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Both were modified, keeping their real shapes and proportions. The Wayfarer keeps the
I-PACE's body panels, glass, lamps and wheels, decimated and recoloured with flat role
colours. Its interior, brakes, lamp internals, grille mesh, "I-PACE" script, Jaguar
badges and plate are removed. The Cyber Cab's single generated mesh is made symmetric,
its mirrors, plate recess and baked-in wheels are removed, and it is remeshed and
decimated, with its colour regions cut along planes (its texture is not used).
They are parodies and are not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Jaguar or Waymo.

its grille panel, teal swoosh and lamp outlines, the Cyber Cab's wheels, arch liners and
light bars, and both cars' ink seam lines are our own.
