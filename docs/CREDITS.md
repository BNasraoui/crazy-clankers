## 3D models

The robotaxis are built from these Sketchfab models, used under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Both were modified: decimated, cleaned up (interiors, wheels, small parts removed),
de-branded (every logo, badge, name and licence plate removed), recoloured with flat
materials, and given new wheels and details. They are parodies and are not affiliated
with or endorsed by Tesla, Jaguar or Waymo.
